DCIM export

Portrayal is the source of truth for the physical facts; a DCIM is one consumer of them. This is the file spec/tools/portrayal/nautobot_export.py writes at build time, in the schema used by nautobot/devicetype-library, so a device modelled here loads into Nautobot without anyone re-typing port counts by hand. Interface names come from the NOS profile, because they are a property of the network OS rather than of the hardware.
