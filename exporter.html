DCIM export

Portrayal is the source of truth for the physical facts; a DCIM is one consumer of them. This is what spec/tools/portrayal/dcim_export.py writes at build time, in the schema shared by netbox-community/devicetype-library and nautobot/devicetype-library. One device type per SKU, so an AC front-to-back and a DC back-to-front are separate entries with their own part numbers and images. Interface names come from a NOS profile where one applies — they are a property of the network OS rather than the hardware — and a device that runs no NOS still gets a device type.
