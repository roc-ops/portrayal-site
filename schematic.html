Device schematics prototype

The compiled drawing is a footprint — true millimetres, every part where it really is. This is the schematic: a compact block with every port as a stub on the outside edge, so a cable never has to cross the device to reach a port. Bands are the device's own bays where it has them — swap a card and the band follows — and its port groups where it does not. Every stub keeps the port's real id.
