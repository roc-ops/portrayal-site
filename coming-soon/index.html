A picture is worth a thousand words.
An addressable picture answers questions.

Every physical thing,
individually addressable.

Portrayal turns a YAML device manifest into a model in real millimeters, where every port, PSU, fan, LED, bay and region is a named thing with a stable id. One model becomes whatever the job needs: an SVG you can color from telemetry, a device type in your DCIM, a shape library, a solid you can turn over in 3D.
