Device facts

Pick devices to read what the library states about them side by side. Every cell says where its figure comes from: derived is counted or measured off the drawing or the chassis, stated is read from the model. A figure nobody has put in the model reads not modelled; that is not zero, and it is not a statement that the device lacks it.
