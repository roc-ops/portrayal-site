One row per component major: what it is, how big it is in millimeters (w × h × d), the standard it conforms to or the interface it presents or mates, how many devices seat it and how many other components compose it. A device seats a part when a placement or bay places it, a bay accepts it or a configuration puts it in one; naming it in prose does not count.
