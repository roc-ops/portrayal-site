What the linter checks, and why.

Every rule the library has to pass before a drawing is accepted: what it applies to, what it checks, why it exists, what to do about a finding, and whether a finding fails the lint or only warns. Rendered from the linter's own rule table rather than transcribed, so this page lists exactly the rules the linter runs. A device that claims maturity: verified is held to more of them than one still being drawn.
