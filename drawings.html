Download drawings

The drawings themselves, as they are compiled — flat SVG in real millimetres, every port, PSU, fan, LED, bay and region carrying a stable id and a data-path. No exporter in the middle: this is what the other exports are made from, and what you would build your own from.

Zip of the compiled SVGs

every configuration
a device is drawn once per configuration — AC and DC, front-to-back and back-to-front. Off, you get the default one; on, you get all of them, which is most of the bytes.
manifest
index.json naming every file with its device, configuration and face, so a script does not have to parse filenames, plus each device's own configs.json.

What comes out:

devices/<device>/<device>.<config>.<face>.svg
components/<vendor>--<part>--<version>--<skin>.svg
